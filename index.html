HOW TO READ IT

Three numbers. One market picture.

Price shows what PI is trading for in USD across the selected market source.

Market cap estimates the value of circulating PI at the reference price. It is not the total cash invested in Pi.

24-hour volume measures the value of trades over a rolling day. Higher volume can mean more activity, but does not indicate direction by itself.
